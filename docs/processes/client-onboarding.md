## 🎯 Onboarding Goals

**Immediate (Day 1):**
- Klient czuje się excited i confident
- Clear next steps
- Communication setup

**Week 1:**
- Projekt setup kompletny
- Kickoff meeting przeprowadzony
- Discovery w toku lub zakończony
- Klient zna team i proces

**Week 2:**
- Wszystkie materiały zebrane
- Brief finalized
- Praca już widoczna startuje

### Stage 2: Pre-Kickoff Preparation

```markdown
# Pre-Kickoff Checklist

Please gather these materials before our kickoff meeting:

## Brand Assets
- [ ] Logo files (vector if possible)
- [ ] Brand guidelines (if exist)
- [ ] Color codes
- [ ] Fonts used

## Content
- [ ] Existing website content (if applicable)
- [ ] Marketing materials
- [ ] Key messaging
- [ ] Product/service descriptions

## Inspiration & References
- [ ] Websites you love (and why)
- [ ] Websites you dislike (and why)
- [ ] Competitor sites to review

## Technical
- [ ] Current website access (if applicable)
- [ ] Domain & hosting info
- [ ] Analytics access (Google Analytics, etc.)
- [ ] Any existing technical documentation

## Strategic
- [ ] Any user research or data
- [ ] Business goals & KPIs
- [ ] Target audience information

## Team
- [ ] Who will be our main points of contact?
- [ ] Who needs to approve deliverables?
- [ ] Who can provide quick feedback?

Don't worry if you don't have everything - we'll work through it together!
```

**Internal Prep:**
- [ ] Review discovery notes & proposal
- [ ] Research industry/competitors
- [ ] Prepare kickoff presentation
- [ ] Draft project brief/SOW
- [ ] Prepare questions
- [ ] Team aligned on approach

### Stage 3: Kickoff Meeting (90-120 min)

**Agenda** (sent before meeting):

```markdown
# Kickoff Meeting Agenda
[Project Name] - [Date]

## Goals
- Align on vision & goals
- Clarify scope & timeline
- Establish process & communication
- Get to know each other
- Start strong!

## Agenda (90 min)

**Welcome & Introductions** (10 min)
- Team intros
- Ice breaker

**Project Vision** (15 min)
- Goals & success criteria
- Target audience
- Key messages

**Scope & Deliverables** (15 min)
- What we're building
- What's included/excluded
- Clarify any questions from proposal

**Process & Timeline** (20 min)
- How we work
- Milestones & dates
- Feedback & approval process

**Design Direction** (15 min)
- Inspirations
- Brand guidelines
- Design preferences

**Communication & Tools** (10 min)
- How we communicate
- Tool walkthrough
- Response time expectations

**Next Steps & Q&A** (15 min)
- Immediate next steps
- Questions
- Excitement building!

See you there!
```

**Kickoff Execution:**

**1. Welcome & Intros (10 min)**
- [ ] Everyone introduces themselves
- [ ] Role in project
- [ ] Fun fact or icebreaker
- [ ] Set positive tone

**2. Project Vision (15 min)**
- [ ] Review business goals
- [ ] Discuss target audience
- [ ] Align on success criteria
- [ ] "What does home run look like?"

**3. Scope & Deliverables (15 min)**
- [ ] Walk through proposal scope
- [ ] Clarify any gray areas
- [ ] Confirm inclusions/exclusions
- [ ] Set expectations clearly

**4. Process & Timeline (20 min)**
- [ ] Explain our process (Discovery → Design → Dev → Launch)
- [ ] Show timeline with milestones
- [ ] Explain feedback loops
- [ ] Set deadlines & dependencies
- [ ] Review approval process

**5. Design Direction (15 min)**
- [ ] Review brand assets
- [ ] Discuss inspirations
- [ ] Set design expectations
- [ ] Understand preferences & constraints

**6. Communication & Tools (10 min)**
- [ ] Communication channels (Slack for daily, email for formal, meetings for big decisions)
- [ ] Response time expectations
- [ ] Meeting cadence (e.g., weekly check-ins)
- [ ] Tool walkthrough

**7. Next Steps & Q&A (15 min)**
- [ ] Recap immediate next steps
- [ ] Assign action items (both sides)
- [ ] Answer all questions
- [ ] Build excitement!

**After Kickoff:**
- [ ] Send meeting notes & recording
- [ ] Send updated timeline
- [ ] Assign action items in PM tool
- [ ] Schedule next meeting

## 🎯 Success Criteria

**Good Onboarding:**
- ✅ Client feels confident & excited
- ✅ Clear understanding of process
- ✅ All materials gathered
- ✅ Communication flowing
- ✅ No confusion or uncertainty
- ✅ Work starts smoothly

**Red Flags:**
- 🚩 Client seems confused
- 🚩 Missing critical materials
- 🚩 Unclear scope
- 🚩 Communication not working
- 🚩 Misaligned expectations

**Fix quickly if red flags appear!**

---

## 💡 Onboarding Tips

**Do:**
- ✅ Overcommunicate early
- ✅ Set clear expectations
- ✅ Be patient & educational
- ✅ Build relationship, not just transactional
- ✅ Make them feel special
- ✅ Start strong with quick win

**Don't:**
- ❌ Assume they know process
- ❌ Info dump overwhelming details
- ❌ Skip kickoff ("we'll figure it out")
- ❌ Be too casual or too formal
- ❌ Let questions go unanswered
- ❌ Delay start

## 🔄 Communication Norms

**Establish Early:**

**Slack:**
- Daily communication
- Quick questions
- Informal updates
- Working hours: [Define, e.g., 9-18 CET]
- Response time: Same day

**Email:**
- Formal communication
- Approvals & sign-offs
- Stakeholder updates
- Meeting summaries

**Meetings:**
- Weekly check-ins (30 min)
- Review sessions (60 min)
- Ad-hoc when needed
- Always with agenda

**Response Expectations:**
- Us to client: Same business day
- Client to us: 2-3 business days for feedback
- Urgent items: Flag and get faster response
