# Hiring Process - important.is

Process rekrutacji od identyfikacji potrzeby po onboarding nowego członka zespołu.

---

## 🎯 Hiring Philosophy

### Hire for:
- ✅ **Values alignment** - Important over Urgent, Quality, Learning
- ✅ **Thoughtfulness** - strategic thinking
- ✅ **Craftsmanship** - pride in work
- ✅ **Growth mindset** - continuous learning
- ✅ **Communication** - clear, proactive
- ✅ **Cultural fit** - partnership mentality

### Not just for:
- ❌ Skills alone (można nauczyć)
- ❌ Years of experience (quality > quantity)
- ❌ Resume prestige

---

## 📋 Hiring Stages

### Stage 1: Identify Need

**Questions:**
- Dlaczego potrzebujemy kogoś?
- Jakiej roli konkretnie?
- Full-time, part-time, contractor?
- Jaki budget?
- Kiedy potrzebujemy?
- Co będzie robić (zakres)?

**Document:**
- Position description
- Responsibilities
- Requirements (must-have vs nice-to-have)
- Compensation range
- Start date goal

---

### Stage 2: Job Posting

**Components:**
- **Hook** - Co czyni tę rolę exciting?
- **About important.is** - Kim jesteśmy, misja
- **Role overview** - Co będziesz robić
- **Responsibilities** - Day-to-day
- **Requirements** - Must-have skills/traits
- **Nice-to-have** - Bonus skills
- **What we offer** - Comp, benefits, culture
- **How to apply** - Process

**Where to Post:**
- 💼 LinkedIn
- 🌐 Important.is website /careers
- 📱 Twitter/X
- 💬 Relevant Slack/Discord communities
- 👥 Network (employees, friends)
- 🎯 Niche job boards (WP community, design, etc.)

**Template:**
```markdown
# [Role Title] at important.is

## We're looking for...
[Exciting opener - what makes role special]

## About important.is
Agencja pełnego cyklu która łączy product thinking z WordPress excellence. Tworzymy rozwiązania które naprawdę mają znaczenie (są important).

## What you'll do
- [Responsibility 1]
- [Responsibility 2]
- [Responsibility 3]

## What we're looking for
Must-have:
- [Skill/trait 1]
- [Skill/trait 2]
- [Skill/trait 3]

Nice-to-have:
- [Bonus 1]
- [Bonus 2]

## What we offer
- Competitive compensation
- Flexible work (remote/hybrid)
- Learning budget
- Great tools
- Meaningful work
- Growth opportunities

## How to apply
Email [email] with:
- Your CV/portfolio
- Brief note why you're interested
- Example of work you're proud of
- [Specific question to filter]

We review applications rolling. Early applications have advantage!
```

### Stage 3: Initial Screening

**Resume/Portfolio Review:**
- Does experience match requirements?
- Quality of work (for design/dev)
- Communication quality (cover letter)
- Red flags (job hopping, gaps explained?)
- Values alignment signals

**Screening Criteria:**
- Must-haves met? ✅/❌
- Portfolio quality (if applicable): 1-5
- Communication quality: 1-5
- Gut feel: Good/Maybe/No

**Action:**
- **No:** Polite rejection email
- **Maybe:** Keep in pool, revisit
- **Yes:** Schedule screening call

---

### Stage 4: Screening Call (30 min)

**Goals:**
- Verify basics
- Assess communication
- Check interest & fit
- Explain process

**Format:**
- 👋 Intros (5 min)
- 💼 Their background (10 min)
- 🏢 About us & role (10 min)
- ❓ Q&A (5 min)

**Questions:**
- Walk me through your background
- What are you looking for in next role?
- Why important.is?
- What kind of work energizes you?
- What's your ideal work environment?
- Availability & timeline?
- Compensation expectations?

**Red Flags:**
- Poor communication
- Misaligned expectations
- Not interested in our values
- Just looking for any job
- Compensation way off

**Green Flags:**
- Clear communication
- Genuine interest in important.is
- Aligned values visible
- Thoughtful questions
- Good energy/chemistry

**Decision:**
- Move forward to skills assessment? Yes/No
- If yes: Send take-home or schedule interview

---

### Stage 5: Skills Assessment

**Options based on role:**

**Developers:**
- Take-home coding challenge (4-6h)
- Or: Live coding session (1-2h)
- Focus: Clean code, problem solving, WordPress/Bricks knowledge

**Designers:**
- Design challenge (4-6h)
- Or: Portfolio deep dive + critique session
- Focus: Process, thinking, execution

**Example Developer Challenge:**
"Build a custom Bricks Builder element that [specific requirement]. Focus on clean code, performance, and user experience."

**Example Designer Challenge:**
"Design 2-3 key pages for [brief]. Show your process and thinking."

**Evaluation:**
- Technical skill level
- Problem-solving approach
- Code/design quality
- Attention to detail
- Communication (documentation, presentation)

---

### Stage 6: Culture & Values Interview (60-90 min)

**Panel:**
- Founder/Lead
- Potential peer
- (Small team: maybe just founder)

**Format:**
- 👋 Warm up (5 min)
- 🎯 Values questions (30-40 min)
- 💼 Skills deep dive (20-30 min)
- ❓ Their questions (15-20 min)
- 📋 Next steps (5 min)

**Values Questions:**

**Important over Urgent:**
- "Tell me about time you pushed back on urgent but not important request"
- "How do you prioritize when everything seems urgent?"

**Quality & Craftsmanship:**
- "Show me work you're most proud of. Why?"
- "Tell me about time you refactored/improved something that worked"

**Continuous Learning:**
- "What have you learned in last 3 months?"
- "How do you stay current in your field?"

**Transparent Partnership:**
- "Tell me about project that went wrong. How did you handle it?"
- "How do you give/receive feedback?"

**Thoughtful:**
- "Walk me through your decision process for [recent project]"
- "Tell me about time you questioned common approach"

**Skills Deep Dive:**
- Discuss take-home challenge
- Or: Portfolio/work examples deep dive
- Technical/design questions specific to role
- Problem-solving scenarios

**Assess:**
- Values alignment
- Cultural fit
- Technical capability
- Growth potential
- Team chemistry
- Communication

---

### Stage 7: Reference Checks

**Ask candidate for 2-3 references:**
- Previous managers/clients
- Colleagues/peers
- (Not current employer if still employed)

**Questions for References:**
- How did you work with [candidate]?
- What were their strengths?
- Areas for improvement?
- How did they handle challenges/feedback?
- Would you work with them again?
- Anything else we should know?

**Validate:**
- Work quality
- Collaboration
- Values fit
- No red flags

---

### Stage 8: Decision & Offer

**Internal Decision:**
- Team debrief
- Consensus building
- Gut check
- If yes: Prepare offer

**Offer Components:**
- 💰 Compensation (salary/hourly)
- 📅 Start date
- 🏢 Employment type (full-time, contractor, etc.)
- 📍 Location (remote, hybrid, office)
- 🎁 Benefits
- 📋 Responsibilities
- ⏰ Working hours/expectations
- 📚 Learning budget/allowances
- 🔧 Tools provided
- ⏱️ Trial period (if applicable)

**Offer Call:**
- Verbally extend offer
- Walk through details
- Answer questions
- Express excitement!
- Send formal offer letter
- Give deadline to respond (e.g., 7 days)

**Negotiation:**
- Be open but fair
- Know your limits
- Find win-win if possible

## 📊 Hiring Metrics

**Track:**
- Time to hire (from posting to acceptance)
- Applicants per role
- Screen rate (% moving to call)
- Interview rate (% moving to interview)
- Offer rate (% receiving offer)
- Acceptance rate
- Quality of hire (assess after 3-6 months)
- Source of hire (where did they find us)

**Goals:**
- Time to hire: < 45 days
- Offer acceptance: > 80%
- Quality of hire: > 4/5 after 6 months
- Retention: > 90% after 1 year

---

## 🚫 Common Hiring Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Hiring out of desperation
- ❌ Ignoring culture fit for skills
- ❌ Not checking references
- ❌ Unclear role definition
- ❌ Moving too fast (or too slow)
- ❌ Not involving team
- ❌ Overselling role
- ❌ Underselling compensation
