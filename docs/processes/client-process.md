# Client Process - important.is

Kompletny proces pracy z klientem od pierwszego kontaktu do długoterminowego partnerstwa.

---

## 📋 Process Overview

```
Lead → Discovery → Proposal → Contract → Kickoff → Execution → Delivery → Maintenance
```

**Typical Timeline:**
- Lead to Discovery: 0-7 dni
- Discovery to Proposal: 2-5 dni
- Proposal to Contract: 7-14 dni
- Contract to Kickoff: 3-7 dni
- Kickoff to Delivery: 4-12 tygodni (projekt dependent)
- Ongoing Maintenance: continuous

---

## Stage 1: Lead 🎯

### Objective
Qualify lead i umówić discovery call.

### Sources
- 🌐 Website inquiry
- 💼 LinkedIn message
- 📧 Email referral
- 🤝 Networking event
- 📱 Social media
- 👥 Direct referral

### Actions

**Within 24h:**
1. **Respond promptly** (email/message)
   - Thank you for reaching out
   - Brief intro to important.is
   - Ask qualifying questions
   - Propose discovery call

**Qualifying Questions (email):**
- What's the project about? (brief description)
- What's driving this now? (timing/urgency)
- What's your timeline?
- What's your approximate budget range?
- How did you find us?

**Template Email Response:**
```
Subject: Re: [Their subject] - Let's talk!

Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out to important.is!

We'd love to help with [their project]. We specialize in [relevant to their need] and have experience with [relevant industry/type].

To better understand your needs and see if we're a good fit, I'd love to schedule a brief discovery call (30-45 minutes).

Before we chat, a few quick questions:
- What's driving this project now?
- What's your ideal timeline?
- Do you have a budget range in mind?

[Calendar link] - pick a time that works for you
Or let me know your availability and I'll send an invite.

Looking forward to learning more!

Best,
[Name]
important.is
```

### Qualification Criteria

**Green Flags (good fit):**
- ✅ Budget aligns (15,000+ PLN)
- ✅ Timeline reasonable (6+ weeks)
- ✅ Clear business goal
- ✅ Decision maker reaching out or involved
- ✅ Values quality
- ✅ Audience match (startup, established, agency)

**Red Flags (maybe not fit):**
- ❌ Extreme budget constraints (< 10,000 PLN)
- ❌ Unrealistic timeline (full site in 2 weeks)
- ❌ Unclear goals ("I need website")
- ❌ Many decision makers, długi proces
- ❌ Price shopping only
- ❌ Bad vibes/red flags in communication

**Action:**
- Green: Prioritize, book discovery ASAP
- Yellow: Explore in discovery, be honest
- Red: Politely decline or redirect

### Tools
- 📧 Email / Gmail
- 📅 Calendly / Calendar booking
- 💼 CRM (Notion, HubSpot, etc.) - track leads
- 📝 Lead qualification form

### Success Metrics
- Response time < 24h
- Lead to discovery booking rate > 60%
- Qualified leads > 70%

## Stage 2: Discovery Call 🔍

### Objective
Deeply understand klienta, problem, goals. Determine fit. Exciting partnership.

### Preparation (Before Call)

**Research (15-30 min):**
- 🌐 Check their current website
- 💼 LinkedIn company & people
- 🔍 Google business, news
- 📊 Competitors quick look
- 📝 Prepare questions

**Setup:**
- 📅 Calendar invite with Zoom/Meet link
- 📧 Confirmation email with agenda
- 📝 Notetaking ready (Notion, etc.)
- 🎥 Camera, mic tested

### During Call (45-60 min)

**Introduction (5-10 min):**
- 👋 Greetings & rapport
- 📋 Agenda overview
- 🎯 Set expectation (today we learn, then propose)

**Discovery Questions (30-40 min):**

**Business Context:**
- Tell me about your business?
- What do you do? For whom?
- How do you make money?
- What's your current situation?
- What's working well? What's not?

**Project Why:**
- What's driving this project now?
- What problem are we solving?
- What happens if you don't do this?
- Who cares about this internally?

**Goals & Success:**
- What does success look like?
- How will you measure it?
- What business impact do you expect?
- What would make this a home run?

**Audience & Users:**
- Who's the target audience?
- What do they need?
- What's the user journey?
- What should they do on site?

**Scope & Requirements:**
- What do you envision?
- Must-haves vs nice-to-haves?
- Any specific features/functionality?
- Technical constraints or requirements?
- Content - who creates, do you have it?

**Design & Brand:**
- Existing brand guidelines?
- Design preferences/inspiration?
- Websites you love/hate and why?

**Timeline & Budget:**
- What's your ideal timeline?
- Any hard deadlines (launch, event)?
- What's your budget range?
- Is this approved or needs approval?

**Decision Process:**
- Who's involved in decision?
- What's the approval process?
- Are you talking to other agencies?
- What matters most in choosing partner?

**Important Framing:**
- 🧠 Ask "why" to go deeper
- 👂 Listen 70%, talk 30%
- 📝 Take detailed notes
- 🎯 Look for business goals, not just features
- 💡 Share strategic thoughts (show value early)
- 🤝 Assess chemistry and fit

**Our Introduction (5-10 min):**
- 🏢 About important.is (brief!)
- 🎯 Our approach & philosophy
- 💼 Relevant case studies/experience
- 🔧 How we work
- ❓ Answer their questions

**Next Steps (5 min):**
- 📊 "Based on what I heard, I think we can help"
- 📝 "I'll prepare detailed proposal"
- 📅 "You'll have it by [date]"
- 🎥 "Any questions in meantime, just ask"
- 🙏 "Thanks for your time, excited to work together"

### Post-Call (Same Day)

**Thank You Email:**
```
Hi [Name],

Great talking with you today! Really excited about [project].

As discussed, I'll prepare a detailed proposal covering:
- Strategic approach for [their goals]
- Scope & deliverables
- Timeline
- Investment

You'll have it by [date].

In the meantime, [anything they need to send/do].

Thanks again!

[Your name]
```

**Internal:**
- 📝 Write up detailed notes
- 💡 Initial ideas & approach thoughts
- 🎯 Determine if good fit
- 💰 Ballpark pricing
- 📅 Schedule proposal prep time

### Decision Point

**Should we propose?**

**Yes if:**
- ✅ Good alignment (budget, timeline, values)
- ✅ Clear we can help
- ✅ Good chemistry
- ✅ Excited about project
- ✅ Decision maker engaged

**No if:**
- ❌ Budget/timeline impossible
- ❌ Not our expertise
- ❌ Red flags (unrealistic, difficult)
- ❌ Better fit elsewhere

**If No:**
- 💬 Honest conversation
- 🤝 Recommend alternatives
- 🚪 Leave door open

### Tools
- 🎥 Zoom / Google Meet
- 📝 Notion / Note-taking
- 📋 Discovery question template
- 🎬 Recording (if permitted, for notes)

### Success Metrics
- Call completion rate > 95%
- Avg call duration 45-60 min
- Discovery → Proposal rate > 80%
- Client feedback on discovery positive

## Stage 3: Proposal 📊

### Objective
Present clear, compelling proposal that wins projekt.

### Preparation (1-3 days)

**Strategize:**
- 🎯 Review discovery notes
- 💡 Brainstorm approach
- 🔍 Additional research if needed
- 💰 Pricing strategy
- 📋 Scope definition

**Components to Prepare:**
1. Executive Summary
2. Understanding (their problem/goals)
3. Strategic Approach
4. Scope & Deliverables
5. Timeline & Milestones
6. Investment (Pricing)
7. Why important.is
8. Terms & Conditions
9. Next Steps

### Proposal Structure

**1. Executive Summary**
- Project name
- Client name
- Brief overview (3-4 sentences)
- Total investment
- Timeline
- Valid until date

**2. Our Understanding**
- Your business
- The challenge
- Your goals
- Success metrics
- (Shows we listened!)

**3. Strategic Approach**
- Our recommended approach
- Why this approach
- Key strategic considerations
- How we'll achieve goals
- (Shows we think strategically!)

**4. Scope & Deliverables**

Detailed breakdown:
- **Discovery & Strategy**
  - Competitive analysis
  - User research
  - Content strategy
  - Sitemap & IA

- **Design**
  - UX/UI design
  - X unique page designs
  - Mobile responsive
  - Design system basics

- **Development**
  - WordPress + Bricks Builder
  - Custom functionality [list]
  - Integrations [list]
  - Performance optimization
  - SEO basics

- **Content**
  - Content migration
  - Copywriting [if applicable]

- **Testing & Launch**
  - QA testing
  - Client training
  - Launch support

- **Post-Launch**
  - 30-day support
  - Documentation

**What's NOT Included:**
- (Be clear on exclusions)

**5. Timeline & Milestones**

```
Week 1-2: Discovery & Strategy
Week 3-4: Design (Iteration 1-2)
Week 5-6: Design Finalization
Week 7-9: Development
Week 10-11: Content, Testing, QA
Week 12: Launch & Training

Total: ~12 weeks
```

**Dependencies:**
- Timely client feedback
- Content delivery by [date]
- Access to necessary systems

**6. Investment**

**Option A: Full Project**
- Total: XX,XXX PLN
- Payment schedule:
  - 40% on contract signing
  - 30% on design approval
  - 30% on launch

**Option B: Phased Approach** (if applicable)
- Phase 1: MVP (XX,XXX PLN)
- Phase 2: Full (XX,XXX PLN)

**Ongoing Maintenance** (Optional)
- Monthly: X,XXX PLN
- Includes: [list]

**7. Why important.is**
- Product thinking approach
- AI-augmented efficiency
- Quality craftsmanship
- Relevant case study highlight
- Team expertise
- Partnership approach

**8. Terms & Conditions**
- Payment terms
- Revision policy
- IP & ownership
- Cancellation policy
- Assumptions

**9. Next Steps**
- Review proposal
- Questions? Let's discuss
- Accept & sign
- Pay deposit
- Kickoff in X days

### Proposal Delivery

**Email:**
```
Subject: Proposal for [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

Attached is our proposal for [project].

We're excited about the opportunity to help you [achieve goal].

The proposal covers:
- Our strategic approach
- Detailed scope
- Timeline
- Investment

I'm happy to walk through it together if helpful - just let me know!

Looking forward to your thoughts.

Best,
[Name]
```

**Format:**
- 📄 PDF (professional, branded)
- 🔗 Optional: Interactive (Notion, Better Proposals)
- 📧 Summary in email body

**Follow-up:**
- Wait 2-3 days
- Check if received/reviewed
- Offer to discuss
- Answer questions
- Address concerns

### Pricing Strategy

**Base Calculation:**
- Estimate hours needed
- x hourly rate (for budgeting)
- + 20-30% buffer
- Consider value to client

**Pricing Tiers:**
- Basic: 15,000-25,000 PLN (5-15 pages, basic custom)
- Standard: 25,000-50,000 PLN (larger, more custom)
- Premium: 50,000-100,000+ PLN (complex, strategic)

**Factors Increasing Price:**
- Complexity
- Custom functionality
- Integrations
- Content work
- Timeline urgency
- Strategic consulting included

**Factors Decreasing Price:**
- Repeat client
- Strategic opportunity
- Capacity filling
- Referral source

### Handling Objections

**"It's too expensive"**
- Focus on value, not cost
- Break down what's included
- Show ROI
- Offer phased approach
- Be willing to walk away

**"Timeline too long"**
- Explain why (quality takes time)
- Show dependencies
- Offer crash option (higher price)
- Be realistic

**"Need to think about it"**
- Totally fine!
- What concerns do you have?
- What would help you decide?
- Set follow-up date

### Tools
- 📝 Proposal template (Notion, Google Docs)
- 🎨 Design template (branded PDF)
- 💼 Proposal software (Better Proposals, PandaDoc)
- 📊 Pricing calculator
- ✍️ E-signature (DocuSign, PandaDoc)

### Success Metrics
- Proposal delivery within promised time
- Win rate > 40%
- Average deal size growth
- Proposal to questions (engagement)

## Stage 4: Contract & Payment 📄

### Objective
Formalize agreement, collect payment, prepare to start.

### Contract Components

**Essential Terms:**
- 👥 Parties (client & important.is)
- 📋 Scope (ref proposal)
- 💰 Price & payment schedule
- 📅 Timeline & milestones
- 🔄 Revision policy (e.g., 2 rounds included)
- 📝 Content responsibility
- ⏱️ Client feedback timelines
- 🔧 Hosting & maintenance (who)
- 🎯 Ownership & IP rights
- 🚫 Cancellation terms
- ⚖️ Liability limitations
- 📧 Communication & contacts
- ✍️ Signatures

**Templates:**
- Standard project contract
- Retainer agreement
- Statement of Work (SOW) for add-ons

### Process

1. **Acceptance**
   - Client verbally accepts
   - Confirm understanding
   - Clarify any last questions

2. **Contract Sent**
   - Send contract for review
   - Highlight key terms
   - Available for questions

3. **Negotiation** (if needed)
   - Minor adjustments OK
   - Major changes = re-proposal
   - Find win-win

4. **Signature**
   - E-signature via DocuSign/PandaDoc
   - Both parties sign
   - Fully executed copy to both

5. **Payment**
   - Invoice for deposit (usually 40%)
   - Payment via transfer/Stripe
   - Confirm receipt
   - Project starts!

### Payment Terms

**Standard Schedule:**
- 40% - Contract signing
- 30% - Design approval
- 30% - Launch

**Alternative:**
- 50% - Start
- 50% - Launch

**Retainer:**
- Monthly in advance
- Auto-billing

**Methods:**
- Bank transfer
- Stripe (card)
- PayPal (if needed)

### Kickoff Scheduling

**Once contract signed + payment received:**
- 📅 Schedule kickoff meeting
- 📧 Send onboarding email
- 🔧 Setup tools & access
- 📋 Share onboarding checklist

### Tools
- ✍️ DocuSign / PandaDoc
- 💳 Stripe / PayPal
- 💼 Accounting software
- 📄 Contract templates

### Success Metrics
- Contract signing rate > 90% after accepted proposal
- Payment collection time < 7 days
- Contract negotiation required < 30%
