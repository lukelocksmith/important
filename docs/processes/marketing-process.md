## 📊 Marketing Channels

### Priority Channels (Focus Here)

#### 1. LinkedIn 💼
**Why:** B2B audience, credibility building, thought leadership

**Strategy:**
- Personal brands (founders/team) > company page
- Value-first content
- Engagement with community
- Consistency > virality

**Content Types:**
- Insights & lessons learned
- Behind-the-scenes
- Client wins (with permission)
- Industry observations
- How-to's & tips
- Contrarian takes (thoughtfully)

**Cadence:**
- Personal: 3-5x per week
- Company: 2-3x per week

**Tactics:**
- Comment on relevant posts daily
- Network building via DMs
- Share others' good content
- Use storytelling format

---

#### 2. Case Studies & Portfolio 📊
**Why:** Proof, credibility, SEO, sales tool

**Strategy:**
- Quality > quantity
- Tell story (problem → solution → results)
- Business outcomes focus
- Permission dari clients

**Format:**
- Long-form (website)
- Short-form (LinkedIn carousel)
- Video (when possible)
- Before/after visuals

**Structure:**
```markdown
# [Project Name]: [Compelling Headline]

## Client
- Company & industry
- Size/stage
- Their business

## Challenge
- What problem they had
- Why it mattered
- What they tried before

## Approach
- Our strategy
- Why we chose it
- Process highlights

## Solution
- What we built
- Key features
- Technical highlights

## Results
- Business metrics
- User impact
- Client feedback

## Testimonial
[Client quote]

## Learnings
What we learned
```

**Cadence:**
- 1 detailed case study per month
- Repurpose into multiple formats

---

#### 3. Website & SEO 🌐
**Why:** Inbound leads, credibility, always-on marketing

**Strategy:**
- Showcase expertise
- Clear value proposition
- Case study library
- Helpful content
- SEO optimization

**Key Pages:**
- Homepage: Clear positioning & value
- Services: What we do & for whom
- Portfolio/Case Studies: Proof
- About: Story, team, values
- Blog: Thought leadership
- Contact: Easy to reach

**SEO Focus:**
- "WordPress agency [Poland]"
- "Bricks Builder experts"
- "Product thinking agency"
- Long-tail: "[Industry] website development"

**Content Marketing:**
- Blog posts on expertise topics
- How-to guides
- Industry insights
- Process explanations

**Cadence:**
- 2-4 blog posts per month
- Regular updates to portfolio

---

#### 4. Referral Network 🤝
**Why:** Highest quality leads, warm introductions

**Strategy:**
- Delight every client (NPS > 50)
- Make referrals easy
- Incentivize (optional)
- Build partnerships

**Tactics:**
- Ask for referrals at right moment (after success)
- Provide referral templates/language
- Thank & reward referrers
- Build agency partnerships (white-label)
- Network at events

**Template Ask:**
```
We're grateful for the opportunity to work with you on [project]!

If you know anyone who might benefit from our services (startups, businesses needing web presence, other agencies), we'd appreciate an introduction.

As a thanks, we offer [referral incentive, e.g., discount on future work, referral fee, gift].

Thank you for your trust!
```

## ✍️ Content Creation Process

### 1. Ideation
**Sources:**
- Client work learnings
- Industry trends
- Questions we get asked
- Contrarian observations
- Personal experiences

**Content Bank:**
- Maintain list of ideas (Notion)
- Categorize: Tactical, Strategic, Story, Insight
- Prioritize by value & timeliness

---

### 2. Creation

**LinkedIn Post Process:**
1. Write draft (30 min)
2. Let it sit (few hours or overnight)
3. Edit (10 min)
4. Format for readability
5. Add visual if applicable
6. Schedule/post

**Blog Post Process:**
1. Outline (30 min)
2. Write draft (2-4h)
3. Edit (1h)
4. Add visuals/code examples
5. SEO optimize
6. Publish & promote

**Case Study Process:**
1. Client interview (30 min)
2. Gather assets (screenshots, metrics)
3. Write draft (3-4h)
4. Client review & approval
5. Publish & promote (multi-channel)

---

### 3. Distribution

**Multi-channel Repurposing:**
1. Long-form case study (website)
   → LinkedIn carousel (summary)
   → Twitter thread
   → Email newsletter feature

2. Blog post
   → LinkedIn post (key insight)
   → Twitter thread (breakdown)
   → Email snippet

3. Client win
   → LinkedIn announcement
   → Case study (later)
   → Portfolio addition
   → Testimonial

## 📈 Metrics & KPIs

### Awareness Metrics
- Website traffic (unique visitors/month)
- LinkedIn followers (personal & company)
- Email subscribers
- Social media reach

**Goals:**
- Website: 1,000 → 5,000 visitors/mo (2025-2026)
- LinkedIn: 500 → 2,000 followers (personal)
- Email: 0 → 500 subscribers

---

### Engagement Metrics
- LinkedIn engagement rate
- Blog post views & time on page
- Email open & click rates
- Community participation

**Goals:**
- LinkedIn engagement: > 3%
- Blog avg time: > 3 min
- Email open rate: > 25%

---

### Lead Generation Metrics
- Leads per month (total)
- Inbound leads %
- Lead quality score
- Lead source breakdown

**Goals:**
- Leads/month: 10-15 qualified
- Inbound: 40%+ (from current ~20%)
- Lead quality: > 70% fit criteria

---

### Conversion Metrics
- Discovery call booking rate
- Proposal conversion rate
- Win rate by source
- Average deal size by source

**Goals:**
- Discovery booking: > 60%
- Win rate: > 40%
- Referral win rate: > 70%

---

### Business Impact
- Marketing-sourced revenue
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- LTV:CAC ratio
- ROI by channel

**Goals:**
- Marketing-sourced: 50%+ revenue
- CAC: < 20% of first project value
- LTV:CAC: > 5:1

## 🚀 Marketing Plays

### Play: New Project Launch
1. Soft announcement (LinkedIn)
2. Behind-the-scenes during project
3. Launch announcement
4. Full case study (1 month post-launch)
5. Repurpose insights into content

### Play: Thought Leadership
1. Original insight from experience
2. LinkedIn post (test waters)
3. If resonates → blog post (deep dive)
4. If really resonates → speaking topic

### Play: Cold Outreach (Selective)
1. Identify ideal clients
2. Research thoroughly
3. Personalized, value-first message
4. No generic pitches
5. Follow-up thoughtfully
