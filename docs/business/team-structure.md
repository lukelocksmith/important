# Team Structure & Growth - important.is

Struktura organizacyjna i plan rozwoju zespołu od 1 do 40 pracowników.

---

## 🎯 Philosophy

**Principles:**
- **Quality over quantity** - małe, senior zespoły > duże juniorskie
- **Generalists first, specialists later** - elastyczność w początkowych fazach
- **Flat structure długo jak się da** - minimalna hierarchia
- **Remote-friendly** - distributed team możliwy
- **Culture fit = tech fit** - wartości równie ważne jak umiejętności

---

## 📊 Growth Stages Overview

```
Stage 1: Solo/Duo (1-2 people)          → Revenue: 200-500k PLN
Stage 2: Small Team (3-5 people)        → Revenue: 500k-1.5M PLN
Stage 3: Growing Agency (6-12 people)   → Revenue: 1.5M-3M PLN
Stage 4: Established Agency (13-25)     → Revenue: 3M-6M PLN
Stage 5: Scaled Agency (26-40)          → Revenue: 6M-12M PLN
```

## 🚀 Stage 1: Solo/Duo (1-2 people)

### Timeline: Year 0-1
### Revenue Target: 200,000-500,000 PLN

### Team Composition

**Person 1: Founder / Full-Stack Lead**
- Strategy & sales
- Design (basic to mid)
- Development (WordPress/Bricks)
- Project management
- Client communication
- Everything else

**Person 2 (if applicable): Co-Founder or First Hire**

**Option A: Developer/Designer**
- Complements founder's weaker skill
- Execution focus
- Client support

**Option B: Generalist**
- Can do bit of everything
- Grows with company
- Cultural foundation

### Organizational Structure
```
Founder
  └── Freelancers (as needed)
  └── First hire (if applicable)
```

### Key Challenges
- Founder burnout
- Doing everything
- Inconsistent cash flow
- No backup/coverage

### Success Metrics
- Deliver 15-30 projects/year
- Build portfolio
- Prove business model
- Establish processes

## 👥 Stage 2: Small Team (3-5 people)

### Timeline: Year 1-2
### Revenue Target: 500,000-1,500,000 PLN

### Team Composition (5 people example)

**1. Founder / CEO**
- Strategy & vision
- Sales & client relationships
- Team leadership
- High-level oversight
- Still hands-on when needed

**2. Senior Developer**
- WordPress/Bricks expert
- Lead technical execution
- Code review
- Architecture decisions
- Mentor junior (future)

**3. Designer**
- UI/UX design
- Brand work
- Design systems
- Client presentations

**4. Developer/Designer (Mid-level)**
- Execution support
- Either dev or design focused
- Grows into senior role

**5. Project Manager / Client Success**
- Project coordination
- Client communication
- Timeline management
- Quality assurance
- Sales support

### Organizational Structure
```
Founder/CEO
  ├── Senior Developer
  │     └── Mid Developer
  ├── Designer
  └── PM/Client Success
```

### Roles & Responsibilities

**Founder/CEO (40% billable):**
- Sales & proposals: 30%
- Strategy & leadership: 30%
- Client management: 20%
- Billable work: 20%

**Senior Developer (70% billable):**
- Development: 70%
- Code review: 15%
- Mentoring: 10%
- Architecture: 5%

**Designer (80% billable):**
- Design work: 80%
- Design systems: 10%
- Brand development: 10%

**Mid Developer/Designer (85% billable):**
- Execution: 85%
- Learning: 10%
- Support: 5%

**PM/Client Success (50% billable):**
- Project management: 40%
- Client communication: 30%
- Sales support: 20%
- Documentation: 10%

### Hiring Priorities
1. **First:** Technical lead (Senior Dev or Designer - whichever you lack)
2. **Second:** Complement to first (Dev if hired designer, designer if hired dev)
3. **Third:** Execution support (mid-level)
4. **Fourth:** PM/Client Success (or admin support)

### Key Challenges
- Scaling delivery
- Maintaining quality
- Process standardization
- Team communication
- Founder still overwhelmed

### Success Metrics
- 30-50 projects/year
- 3-5 retainer clients
- Team utilization 70%+
- Client NPS > 50

### Timeline: Year 2-3
### Revenue Target: 1,500,000-3,000,000 PLN

### Team Composition (10 people example)

**Leadership (2):**
- Founder/CEO
- Operations Manager or COO

**Development Team (4):**
- 1x Tech Lead
- 2x Senior Developers
- 1x Mid Developer

**Design Team (2):**
- 1x Design Lead
- 1x Mid/Senior Designer

- 1x Senior PM/Client Success

**Business Development (1):**
- 1x Sales/Marketing Lead

### Organizational Structure
```
CEO/Founder
  ├── Operations Manager/COO
  │     ├── PM/Client Success
  │     └── Admin/Office Manager
  │
  ├── Tech Lead
  │     ├── Senior Developer
  │     ├── Senior Developer
  │     └── Mid Developer
  │
  ├── Design Lead
  │     └── Designer
  │
  └── Sales/Marketing Lead
```

### Department Breakdown

**Development (40% of team):**
- WordPress/Bricks specialists
- Custom development
- Performance optimization
- Code quality

**Design (20% of team):**
- UI/UX
- Brand design
- Design systems
- Client presentations

**Client Success & PM (20% of team):**
- Project management
- Client relationships
- Quality assurance
- Support

**Sales & Marketing (10% of team):**
- Lead generation
- Sales process
- Marketing content
- Partnerships

**Operations & Leadership (10% of team):**
- Strategy
- Operations
- Finance
- HR

### Salary Structure (example, PLN/month gross)

**Leadership:**
- CEO: 20,000-30,000 (+ profit share)
- Operations Manager: 12,000-18,000

**Senior Level:**
- Tech Lead: 15,000-22,000
- Senior Developer: 12,000-18,000
- Design Lead: 12,000-18,000
- Senior Designer: 10,000-15,000
- Senior PM: 10,000-15,000

**Mid Level:**
- Mid Developer: 8,000-12,000
- Mid Designer: 7,000-11,000
- PM: 7,000-11,000

**Junior Level:**
- Junior Developer: 5,000-8,000
- Junior Designer: 5,000-7,000

**Sales/Marketing:**
- Sales Lead: 10,000-15,000 (+ commission)

### Hiring Roadmap (Example for 6→12 growth)

**Hire #6: Senior Developer**
- When: Project overflow consistent
- Why: Increase delivery capacity

**Hire #7: Designer**
- When: Design bottleneck
- Why: Can't keep up with projects

**Hire #8: PM/Client Success**
- When: >5 concurrent projects
- Why: Founder can't manage all

**Hire #9: Senior Developer**
- When: Consistent 8+ projects running
- Why: Scale delivery

**Hire #10: Sales/Marketing Lead**
- When: Founder spending 50%+ on sales
- Why: Founder should focus on leadership

**Hire #11: Operations Manager**
- When: CEO overwhelmed with operations
- Why: CEO focuses on strategy

**Hire #12: Mid Developer**
- When: Senior devs overloaded
- Why: Support execution

### Key Challenges
- Middle management emerging
- Process scalability
- Culture maintenance
- Communication complexity
- Quality control at scale

### Success Metrics
- 60-100 projects/year
- 8-12 retainer clients
- Team utilization 75%+
- Employee retention >80%
- Profit margin 25%+

## 📋 Role Definitions

### Individual Contributor Levels

**Junior (0-2 years)**
- Learning fundamentals
- Needs supervision
- Executes defined tasks
- Growing skills

**Mid (2-4 years)**
- Independent execution
- Some mentoring
- Owns features/components
- Reliable delivery

**Senior (4-7 years)**
- Expert execution
- Mentors others
- Owns projects
- Technical leadership

**Staff/Principal (7+ years)**
- Strategic technical decisions
- Cross-team impact
- Thought leadership
- Innovation driver

### Management Track

**Team Lead**
- 3-6 direct reports
- Technical + people management
- Still hands-on (60%+)

**Manager**
- 6-10 direct reports
- People + process focus
- Less hands-on (30%)

**Director**
- Manages managers
- Department strategy
- Budget ownership
- Minimal hands-on

**VP/C-level**
- Company strategy
- Multiple departments
- Board level
- No hands-on

## 💰 Compensation Philosophy

### Base Principles
- **Market competitive** - within top 40% of market
- **Transparent bands** - clear ranges per level
- **Merit-based** - performance = advancement
- **Profit sharing** - all benefit from success

### Compensation Components

**Base Salary:**
- Market competitive
- Regular reviews (annual)
- Band progression

**Bonus/Profit Share:**
- Company performance
- Individual performance
- Starts at senior level

**Benefits:**
- Health insurance (co-funded)
- Learning budget (20,000 PLN/year)
- Equipment (MacBook, etc.)
- Flexible work
- Paid time off (26+ days)

**Equity/Ownership (Future):**
- Key leadership
- Long tenure employees
- Performance based

### Salary Progression Example

**Developer Track:**
```
Junior:        5,000-8,000 PLN/month
Mid:          8,000-12,000 PLN/month
Senior:      12,000-18,000 PLN/month
Staff:       18,000-25,000 PLN/month
Principal:   25,000-35,000 PLN/month
```

**Raises:**
- Annual performance: 5-15%
- Promotion: 15-30%
- Market adjustment: as needed

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Hiring:**
- ❌ Hiring too fast
- ❌ Lowering bar for speed
- ❌ Culture fit ignored
- ❌ Not firing fast enough

**Structure:**
- ❌ Too much hierarchy too soon
- ❌ Unclear roles
- ❌ Overlapping responsibilities
- ❌ Silos forming

**Compensation:**
- ❌ Below market rates
- ❌ Huge pay gaps
- ❌ No progression path
- ❌ Unfair distribution

**Culture:**
- ❌ Losing startup culture
- ❌ No values enforcement
- ❌ Poor communication
- ❌ Politics emerging
