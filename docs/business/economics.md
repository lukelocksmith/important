## 📊 Unit Economics

### Project Economics

**Average Project (30,000 PLN):**
```
Revenue:              30,000 PLN (100%)
Direct Costs:          6,000 PLN (20%)
  - Team time
  - Tools/licenses
  - Subcontractors (if any)
------------------------
Gross Profit:         24,000 PLN (80%)

Operating Expenses:    9,000 PLN (30%)
  - Salaries/overhead
  - Marketing
  - Operations
  - Tools & software
------------------------
Net Profit:           15,000 PLN (50%)
```

**Target Margins:**
- Gross margin: 70-80%
- Net margin: 30-40% (mature)
- Early stage: 20-30% acceptable

---

### Retainer Economics

**Example Retainer (8,000 PLN/month):**
```
Monthly Revenue:       8,000 PLN
Direct Costs:          2,400 PLN (30%)
  - Team time (~20h/month)
------------------------
Gross Profit:          5,600 PLN (70%)

Operating (allocated):  2,000 PLN (25%)
------------------------
Net Profit:            3,600 PLN (45%)
```

**Benefits:**
- Higher margin (less sales cost)
- Predictable
- Compounds over time

## 💵 Pricing Strategy

### Philosophy
- **Value-based** pricing, not hourly
- Based on business impact, not time
- Transparent ranges
- Flexible engagement models

### Pricing Tiers

**Startup/SMB Projects:**
- 15,000-40,000 PLN
- Faster timelines
- Standard features
- Self-service content

**Mid-Market:**
- 40,000-80,000 PLN
- Custom solutions
- Strategic input
- More hand-holding

**Enterprise/Premium:**
- 80,000-150,000+ PLN
- Comprehensive strategy
- Custom everything
- White-glove service

---

### Pricing Factors

**Increase Price For:**
- ✅ Complexity
- ✅ Tight timeline
- ✅ Custom development
- ✅ Strategic consulting
- ✅ Multiple stakeholders
- ✅ Ongoing support included

**Decrease Price For:**
- ✅ Repeat clients
- ✅ Strategic partnerships
- ✅ Case study opportunities
- ✅ Portfolio building
- ✅ Off-season (if applicable)

## 💰 Cash Flow Management

### Principles
- **Collect deposits:** 40% upfront
- **Milestone payments:** 30% mid-project, 30% on delivery
- **Net-30 payment terms** maximum
- **Maintain 3-6 months runway** always

### Payment Schedule

**Project Example:**
```
Day 0 (Contract):       40% deposit
Day 30 (Design Done):   30%
Day 60 (Launch):        30% final
```

**Retainer:**
```
Monthly in advance
Auto-billing preferred
```

## 📊 Key Financial Metrics

### Track Monthly:
- [ ] Revenue (total, per stream)
- [ ] Gross profit margin
- [ ] Net profit margin
- [ ] Cash balance
- [ ] Accounts receivable aging
- [ ] Revenue per employee
- [ ] Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- [ ] Customer lifetime value (LTV)

### Health Indicators:

**Good:**
- ✅ Gross margin > 70%
- ✅ Net margin > 25% (mature)
- ✅ 3+ months runway
- ✅ LTV:CAC > 3:1
- ✅ Revenue/employee > 500,000 PLN/year

**Warning Signs:**
- 🚩 Gross margin < 60%
- 🚩 Net margin < 15%
- 🚩 < 2 months runway
- 🚩 High accounts receivable
- 🚩 Low capacity utilization
