# Microeconomics - Project Level

Ekonomia na poziomie pojedynczego projektu.

---

## 🎯 Project Economics Framework

### Key Question:
**"Is this project profitable?"**

Variables:
- Revenue (what we charge)
- Direct costs (time, tools, subcontractors)
- Allocated overhead
- Opportunity cost

---

## 💰 Project Profitability Model

### Formula
```
Gross Profit = Revenue - Direct Costs
Net Profit = Gross Profit - Allocated Overhead
Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Revenue) × 100%
```

### Example: 30,000 PLN Project

```
REVENUE:                   30,000 PLN (100%)

DIRECT COSTS:               6,000 PLN (20%)
  Team time (80h × 75 PLN): 6,000 PLN
  Tools/licenses:             500 PLN
  Subcontractors:             0 PLN
  ---------------------------------
GROSS PROFIT:              23,500 PLN (78%)

ALLOCATED OVERHEAD:         7,500 PLN (25%)
  Salaries (non-billable):  4,000 PLN
  Marketing/sales:          1,500 PLN
  Office/ops:              1,000 PLN
  Software:                1,000 PLN
  ---------------------------------
NET PROFIT:                16,000 PLN (53%)
```

**Target:**
- Gross margin: 70-80%
- Net margin: 40-50%

## ⏱️ Time-Based Economics

### Effective Hourly Rate

```
Effective Rate = Total Project Revenue / Total Hours Spent

Example:
30,000 PLN / 80 hours = 375 PLN/hour effective

Target: 300-500 PLN/hour effective
```

### Time Allocation

**Typical Project Breakdown (100 hours):**
```
Discovery:          10h (10%)
Design:             25h (25%)
Development:        45h (45%)
Testing:            10h (10%)
Launch & training:  10h (10%)
```

**Hidden Time (Track!):**
- Sales & proposal: 5-10h
- Project management: 10-15h
- Revisions: 5-10h
- Support post-launch: 3-5h

**Total Real Time:** Often 130-140h vs 100h estimated

## 📊 Project Types Economics

### Small Project (15,000-25,000 PLN)

**Characteristics:**
- 40-60 hours work
- Template-based or simple custom
- 3-4 weeks timeline
- Lower margin (more fixed costs proportion)

**Economics:**
```
Revenue:          20,000 PLN
Direct costs:      4,500 PLN (50h × 90 PLN)
Gross profit:     15,500 PLN (78%)
Overhead:          6,000 PLN (30%)
Net profit:        9,500 PLN (48%)
```

**Good for:**
- Cash flow
- Volume
- Portfolio building
- Repeat clients

---

### Medium Project (25,000-50,000 PLN)

**Characteristics:**
- 80-120 hours
- Custom design & development
- 6-10 weeks
- Best margin typically

**Economics:**
```
Revenue:          35,000 PLN
Direct costs:      7,500 PLN (100h × 75 PLN)
Gross profit:     27,500 PLN (79%)
Overhead:          8,750 PLN (25%)
Net profit:       18,750 PLN (54%)
```

**Sweet spot:**
- Best profit margins
- Manageable complexity
- Good client experience

---

### Large Project (50,000-100,000+ PLN)

**Characteristics:**
- 150-250+ hours
- Complex custom development
- 10-16+ weeks
- Higher gross profit, watch overhead

**Economics:**
```
Revenue:          75,000 PLN
Direct costs:     15,000 PLN (200h × 75 PLN)
Gross profit:     60,000 PLN (80%)
Overhead:         18,750 PLN (25%)
Net profit:       41,250 PLN (55%)
```

**Challenges:**
- Longer sales cycle
- More complexity/risk
- Requires senior team
- Cash flow implications

## 🎚️ Pricing Optimization

### Cost-Plus vs Value-Based

**Cost-Plus (Don't use primarily):**
```
Hours × Rate + Margin = Price
100h × 150 PLN × 1.5 = 22,500 PLN
```

**Value-Based (Preferred):**
```
Client value = 500,000 PLN in leads/sales
Our fee = 50,000 PLN (10% of value)
Our cost = 12,000 PLN (80h)
Margin = 76%
```

### Pricing Sweet Spot

**Find intersection of:**
- Client willingness to pay
- Our costs
- Competitor pricing
- Value delivered
- Market positioning

## 📉 Breakeven Analysis

### Project Breakeven

**Question:** What's minimum we can charge and breakeven?

```
Breakeven Revenue = Direct Costs + Allocated Overhead

Example:
Direct costs:       7,500 PLN
Overhead allocation: 7,500 PLN
Breakeven:         15,000 PLN

Anything above = profit
```

**Use for:**
- Minimum pricing
- Special situations
- Cost estimation

## ⚖️ Risk vs Reward

### High-Profit Scenarios
- ✅ Efficient delivery (less hours than estimated)
- ✅ High perceived value (charge more)
- ✅ Reusable work (amortize past work)
- ✅ Low revisions needed

### Low-Profit Scenarios
- ❌ Scope creep (unbilled hours)
- ❌ Many revisions
- ❌ Technical challenges
- ❌ Difficult client (communication overhead)
- ❌ New technology (learning curve)

### Mitigate Risks:
- Clear scope in contract
- Revision limits
- Change request process
- Buffer in estimates
- Qualify clients well

## 🔄 Repeat Client Economics

### First Project vs Repeat

**First Project:**
```
Revenue:          30,000 PLN
Sales time:       10h × 150 PLN = 1,500 PLN
Net profit:       13,500 PLN (45%)
```

**Repeat Project:**
```
Revenue:          30,000 PLN
Sales time:        2h × 150 PLN = 300 PLN
Net profit:       15,700 PLN (52%)
```

**Repeat Client Benefits:**
- Lower acquisition cost
- Know their brand/needs
- Trust established
- Faster onboarding
- Higher margin

**Target:** 50%+ revenue from repeat clients

## 📊 Project Portfolio Mix

### Optimal Mix (Example)

**Revenue Target: 600,000 PLN/year**

```
10 × Small (20,000 PLN):     200,000 PLN (33%)
10 × Medium (30,000 PLN):    300,000 PLN (50%)
2 × Large (50,000 PLN):      100,000 PLN (17%)
---------------------------------------------
Total:                       600,000 PLN

Weighted margin:             ~50%
Profit:                      300,000 PLN
```

**Balance:**
- Small = cash flow, volume
- Medium = profit, manageability
- Large = prestige, growth

## 💡 Improving Project Economics

### Increase Revenue
- Premium positioning
- Value-based pricing
- Upsell/cross-sell
- Scope optimization

### Decrease Costs
- Process efficiency
- Reusable components
- AI-augmented workflows
- Better estimates

### Reduce Risk
- Better client qualification
- Clear scope documentation
- Milestone-based payments
- Revision limits

---

## 📋 Project Evaluation Checklist

**Before Accepting Project:**
- [ ] Revenue meets minimum threshold (e.g., 15,000 PLN)
- [ ] Estimated margin > 40%
- [ ] Client aligned with values
- [ ] Team has capacity
- [ ] Risk is manageable
- [ ] Payment terms acceptable
- [ ] Excited about project

**If No to multiple:** Decline or negotiate

- Estimated cost vs actual
- Margin expected vs achieved
- Payment schedule adherence

### Learn & Improve:
- Why did estimates differ?
- What caused scope creep?
- How to price better next time?
- What to include in future proposals?

### KPIs per Project:
- Gross margin %
- Net margin %
- Effective hourly rate
- Hours over/under estimate
- Client satisfaction
- On-time delivery
