## 💰 Pricing Philosophy

### Core Principles:
- **Value-based** - price on value delivered, not hours
- **Transparent** - clear ranges, no hidden fees
- **Fair** - win-win for both sides
- **Premium** - quality justifies price
- **Flexible** - options to fit different budgets

**We are NOT:**
- Cheapest option (compete on quality, not price)
- Hourly billing (except special cases)
- One-size-fits-all pricing

## 💡 Pricing Strategy Tips

### When to Increase Price:
- High demand (full capacity)
- Difficult client signals
- Complex project
- Rush timeline
- High perceived value

### When to Be Flexible:
- Strategic opportunity (portfolio, case study)
- Dream client
- Long-term potential
- Repeat client
- Slow period

### Never Compete on Price Alone:
- Focus on value, quality, results
- If they only care about price, not our client
- Race to bottom helps no one
