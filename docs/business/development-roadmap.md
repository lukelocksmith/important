## 📅 2028-2030: Maturity & Evolution

### Potential Paths

**Path 1: Specialized Agency**
- Niche focus (e.g., only SaaS companies)
- Premium boutique (small, high-value)
- 15-20 people, $5M+ revenue

**Path 2: Product Company**
- Transition to product-focused
- Agency funds product development
- Eventually 70% product, 30% services

**Path 3: Acquisition/Exit**
- Build for acquisition
- Merged with larger agency
- Founders transition or stay

**Path 4: Holding/Network**
- Multiple specialized brands
- Each with focus area
- Shared resources & clients

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

### Risk: Market Saturation
**Mitigation:** Differentiate through product thinking, quality, AI

### Risk: Talent Shortage
**Mitigation:** Build reputation, strong culture, competitive compensation

### Risk: Economic Downturn
**Mitigation:** Diverse client base, retainers, multiple revenue streams

### Risk: Technology Shifts
**Mitigation:** Continuous learning, early adoption, diversification

### Risk: Founder Dependence
**Mitigation:** Team development, process documentation, leadership growth
