# Jobs to be Done (JTBD) - important.is

Framework Jobs to be Done pomaga zrozumieć DLACZEGO klienci nas "zatrudniają". Nie chodzi o features czy demographics, ale o job który klient chce wykonać.

## 🎯 JTBD Framework

**Format:**
> Kiedy ___[sytuacja]___, chcę ___[motywacja]___, żebym mógł ___[oczekiwany outcome]___.

## 🚀 Primary Jobs to be Done

### Job #1: "Launch professional web presence quickly"

**When I** potrzebuję profesjonalną obecność online dla mojego biznesu/produktu

**I want to** szybko i sprawnie uruchomić wysokiej jakości stronę

**So I can** zacząć generować leads, budować credibility i rosnąć

**Job Context:**
- Startup launching publicly
- Rebranding existing company
- New product/service launch
- Entering new market
- Fundraising (need professional presence)

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ Launch w 6-8 tygodni
- ✅ Professional i credible wygląd
- ✅ Działa perfectly (no bugs, fast, mobile)
- ✅ Można aktualizować bez developera
- ✅ Brings business results (leads, sales)

**Current Alternatives:**
- DIY (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress theme)
- Freelancer
- Large agency
- Offshore development

**Why important.is wins:**
- ⚡ Fast (AI-augmented workflows)
- 💎 Quality (craftsmanship)
- 🎯 Strategic (product thinking)
- 🤝 Partnership approach
- 💰 Right value/price ratio

**Job Performers:**
- Startup founders
- Marketing managers
- Business owners
- Product managers

## 🎯 Job Stories for Product Development

Using JTBD to guide our service development:

### When developing portfolio/case studies:
**Job story:** When prospects research us, they want to see relevant, impressive work so they can feel confident we can handle their project.

**Action:** Create detailed case studies with business outcomes, not just pretty pictures

---

### When designing proposal process:
**Job story:** When client receives proposal, they want clear understanding of value, scope, and process so they can make confident decision quickly.

**Action:** Structured proposals: problem→solution→process→investment→why us

---

### When planning communication:
**Job story:** When project is in progress, clients want to feel informed and in control so they can trust everything is going well.

**Action:** Regular updates, transparent dashboard, proactive communication

---

### When creating maintenance offering:
**Job story:** When site is live, clients want peace of mind and continuous improvement so they can focus on business.

**Action:** Proactive maintenance packages with regular reports and suggestions

- Show job completion in case studies

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Clients hire us for outcomes, nie features**
   - Focus on what they achieve, not what we do

2. **Competition isn't just agencies**
   - We compete with DIY, status quo, in-house options

3. **Anxiety is real and must be addressed**
   - Proactively tackle fears in marketing & sales

4. **Different audiences, same underlying jobs**
   - Jobs unify diverse customer segments

5. **Job completion = customer success**
   - Measure success by job done, not just deliverables
